## 1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing under GDPR is **Tipza**.

Contact email: [support@tipza.app](mailto:support@tipza.app)

## 2. Data we collect

We may collect the following personal data:

- Email address
- Display name or nickname
- Tip split records and history
- Carry-over amounts
- App usage data (feature usage, crash reports)
- Technical data (IP address, device type, operating system, app version)

## 2.1 Tip split calculations

Tipza records and displays tip split calculations based on information provided by users. Tipza stores calculated values for display and history, and does not collect, hold, or distribute funds. Employers and teams remain responsible for payments, payroll, and tax compliance.

## 3. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data based on:

- Contractual necessity (to provide the service)
- User consent (account creation, analytics where applicable)
- Legitimate interest (app improvement and security)

## 4. How we use your data

- Create and manage user accounts
- Perform and store tip calculations
- Sync data across devices
- Improve app stability and performance
- Provide user support

## 5. Data storage and retention

Data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure and local device storage. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service or until you request deletion.

## 6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with trusted service providers for:

- Authentication
- Analytics

All providers are contractually bound to comply with GDPR.

## 11. This website

Sections 1 to 10 describe the Tipza app. This section describes tipza.app, including the blog.

**No cookies, and no consent banner.** The website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device except a note of which articles you have rated, described below. There is nothing to consent to and nothing to opt out of.

**Audience measurement.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and page performance. It is cookieless, it does not fingerprint your browser, and it does not build a profile of you across sites or visits.

**Interaction events.** Alongside page views we record a short, fixed list of actions: reaching the end of an article, rating an article, clicking an App Store button, switching language, opening a related article, using a table of contents, and following a citation. Each record is the name of the action, the page it happened on and at most one short label — for example which store button was clicked. No identifier is attached, so these records cannot be linked to each other or to you. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is recorded at all.

**Article feedback.** When you answer "Was this article helpful?" we store the article, the language, your answer, and — if you tell us — which of four fixed reasons applied. We also store a one-way hash derived from your IP address, your browser's user agent string and the current date. That value exists only to stop one person voting repeatedly; it cannot be reversed, and because the date is part of it, it stops matching the next day. Your IP address itself is never stored. Your browser remembers which articles you have rated so that the question is not asked twice; you can clear that at any time through your browser's site data settings.

**Legal basis.** Legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: understanding which articles are useful, in a form that does not identify readers.
